The Platform

Africa's natural
diamonds.
The world's attention.

"The platform that showcases and connects Africa's diamond sector and ecosystem to the world — retelling the narrative of natural African diamonds to a new generation, growing consumer demand and the economic and social viability of the industry."

We operate co-located activations at the world's major events, festivals, and exhibitions — placing Africa's natural diamond story where the most influential decision-makers already gather. Beyond events, we facilitate and nurture a year-round pipeline of relationships that converts perception into commercial traction. Central to our mission: retelling the narrative of African natural diamonds to a new, global, and young audience.

Upcoming · Angola · 2027/28
Trade Mission

Angola
Trade Mission

The Angola Trade Mission converts Cannes conversations into in-country commercial outcomes — bringing international partners, buyers, investors, and brand decision-makers directly to Angola's diamond operations, processing facilities, and government stakeholders.

Designed to consolidate relationships established at Cannes and across the platform, the Mission ensures every in-country meeting is purposeful, pre-qualified, and commercially ready.

All platform participants receive priority access and inclusion in the Angola Trade Mission programme.
